Everything about your account lives in one place. Select **Account** (the gear next to your name in the top bar) to open the hub. A sidebar on the left lists the sections; pick one and it opens on the right.

## Sections

- **Preferences** - your display name and how Obol displays information: default currency, language, date and time format, and timezone.
- **Access** - the ways you sign in: your [email addresses](emails.md) and your [passkeys](passkeys.md).

## Preferences

The **Preferences** section shows your current settings as a summary. To change them, select **Edit**, update the values, and save - showing them read-only first means you won't change something by accident.

- **Display name** - what Obol calls you in the top bar. Leave it blank to fall back to your email address - your account is never nameless.
- **Default currency** - the currency your totals are converted to and shown in.
- **Language** - the language Obol's text appears in.
- **Date & time format** - how dates and times are written. Choose **Long**, **Medium**, or **Short** to follow your language's own style at that length, or **YYYY-MM-DD** for a fixed international order. Each option shows the current date and time as an example, so you can see exactly what you're picking. This applies everywhere Obol shows a date.
- **Timezone** - the timezone your dates and renewal days are measured against.

## Access

The **Access** section gathers everything about signing in:

- **Your email addresses** - add, verify, promote, or remove addresses. See [Email addresses](emails.md).
- **Your passkeys** - register, rename, or remove passkeys for faster sign-in. See [Passkeys](passkeys.md).
