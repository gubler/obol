---
title: Email addresses
description: Add a backup email address so you're never locked out - add, verify, make primary, and remove addresses.
---

Obol signs you in with a magic link sent to your email, so your email address *is* your key to the account. Adding a second verified address gives you a backup: if you ever lose access to one mailbox, a magic link to the other still gets you in.

You manage your addresses under **[Account](account.md)** in the top bar, in the **Access** section.

## Your addresses at a glance

Every address on your account is listed with a label:

- **Primary** - the main address for your account. It's where your account identity lives, and it's always verified. You have exactly one.
- **Verified** - a confirmed backup address. You can sign in with a magic link sent to it, and you can make it your primary.
- **Pending verification** - an address you've added but not confirmed yet. It can't be used to sign in until you verify it.

## Add an address

1. Open your **Account** and select **Access**.
2. Type the address in the box and select **Send verification link**.
3. Open that mailbox and click the link to confirm it.

A few things worth knowing:

- If it's been a while since you signed in, the **Access** section shows **Sign in again** instead of the address buttons. Adding, removing, or promoting an address needs a fresh sign-in - see [Signing in again to make changes](account.md#signing-in-again-to-make-changes). Confirming an address from your mailbox is unaffected; that link works on any device, signed in or not.
- The address starts as **Pending verification** and becomes a usable backup only once you click the link.
- The confirmation link is valid for **24 hours**. If it expires, select **Resend link** next to the pending address for a fresh one.
- For your privacy, the confirmation message is the same whether or not the address was available - so the page never reveals whether an address is already in use on another account. If an address is already verified on someone else's account, you simply won't be able to confirm it.

## Verify an address

Open the email Obol sent to the new address and click **Confirm this address**. You'll see a confirmation page, and the address flips to **Verified** on your account. You can click the link from any device - you don't need to be signed in on it.

If the link has expired or been used, request a new one with **Resend link**.

## Make an address your primary

Once an address is verified, you can promote it to primary - handy if you're changing which mailbox is your main one:

1. Open your **Account** and select **Access**.
2. Select **Make primary** next to a verified address and confirm.

Your primary address updates immediately, and you stay signed in on this device. For your security, any *other* devices that were kept signed in will need to sign in again.

## Remove an address

Remove a backup address you no longer want:

1. Open your **Account** and select **Access**.
2. Select **Remove** next to the address and confirm.

You can't remove your primary address - it's your account identity. To retire it, make another verified address your primary first, then remove the old one.

Removing an address only affects sign-in - your subscriptions and everything else stay exactly as they are.
