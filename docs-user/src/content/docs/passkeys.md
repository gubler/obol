---
title: Passkeys
description: Sign in faster with a passkey - add, use, and remove them from your account.
---

A passkey is a faster way to sign in. Instead of waiting for a magic-link email, you unlock Obol with whatever already secures your device - your fingerprint, your face, or your device PIN.

Passkeys are optional. Magic-link email is always available, so you never *have* to set one up, and you can remove a passkey whenever you like.

## What is a passkey?

A passkey is a login credential that lives on your device (your phone, laptop, or a security key) and is protected by your fingerprint, face, or PIN. There's no password to type, remember, or leak. When you sign in, your device proves it's really you and unlocks Obol - the secret never leaves the device and is never sent to Obol.

Because a passkey is tied to your device, it's both quicker and harder to phish than a password.

## Add a passkey

You add a passkey while you're signed in:

1. Sign in as usual (a magic link is fine).
2. Open your **[Account](account.md)** and select **Access**.
3. Select **Add a passkey**.
4. Your device or browser prompts you to confirm with your fingerprint, face, or PIN. Approve it.

That's it - the new passkey appears in your list. It gets a default name like "Passkey #1"; rename it to something you'll recognize (see below).

A couple of things worth knowing:

- Passkeys work only over a secure (HTTPS) connection, which Obol always uses.
- You can add more than one - for example, one on your phone and one on your laptop - so you're never locked out if a device isn't handy.

## Use a passkey to sign in

Once you've added a passkey, the login page offers it:

1. On the login page, select **Sign in with a passkey**, or just tap the email box - your browser may offer your saved passkey right there.
2. Confirm with your fingerprint, face, or PIN.
3. You're in - no email round trip.

If passkeys aren't available on the device you're using, the email sign-in is right there as a fallback.

## Rename a passkey

Give each passkey a name you'll recognize later - "Work laptop", "Personal phone", and so on:

1. Open your **Account** and select **Access**.
2. Change the name in the box next to the passkey.
3. Select **Rename**.

## Remove a passkey

Remove a passkey you no longer use - or one on a device you've lost:

1. Open your **Account** and select **Access**.
2. Select **Remove** next to the passkey and confirm.

Removing a passkey only affects sign-in from that credential. Your subscriptions and everything else stay exactly as they are. If you remove your last passkey, you'll simply sign in with a magic-link email again - and you can always add a new passkey later.
