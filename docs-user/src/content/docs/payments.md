---
title: Payments
description: Record what you've paid, and let Obol track renewals for you.
---

Payments are the record of what you've actually paid against a subscription. Obol can keep this up to date for you automatically, or you can record payments by hand - whichever you prefer.

## Automatic vs. manual

By default, Obol **generates** a payment for each renewal as it comes due and rolls the next renewal date forward. These auto-created payments are marked **generated** - placeholders that assume the subscription's current cost.

You can also take over manually. Recording your own payments - or editing the generated ones - lets you capture what you were really charged.

## Recording a payment

1. Open the subscription and select **Add Payment**.
2. Enter the **amount** and the **paid date** (when the charge actually landed).
3. Save.

Recording a payment moves the subscription's **next renewal** forward by one billing cycle, so the countdown on the dashboard stays accurate even if you paid early or late.

## Confirming generated payments

A generated payment is Obol's best guess. When you know the real figure:

- **Validate** confirms it as-is in one click - it becomes a **verified** payment.
- **Edit** lets you adjust the amount or paid date before confirming.

## Manual mode

If you take payments over manually, automatic generation pauses and the subscription shows a **Manual** badge on the dashboard and its page. To hand renewals back to Obol, record a payment (or edit the subscription) with a future **Next Renewal Date** and tick **Restart automatic payments?**.

Setting a **Next Renewal Date in the past** also switches the subscription to manual generation. This lets you enter a subscription you started paying a while ago without Obol immediately generating a run of catch-up payments; the form shows a note when the date you've picked is in the past.

## Renewals on the 29th, 30th, or 31st

If a subscription renews on a day some months don't have — the 31st, say — Obol shows the renewal on the last day of any shorter month (the 28th or 29th in February) and returns to the 31st the next month that has one. The subscription's page marks the renewal date with a small indicator when it's been adjusted this way, so you know to check your actual bill for the exact charge date.

## Editing and deleting

Every payment can be edited (to fix the amount or date) or deleted. A subscription's full payment record is on its page, under **History**.
