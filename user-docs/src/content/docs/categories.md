---
title: Categories
description: Group subscriptions with a color and icon.
---

Categories group related subscriptions - Streaming, Software, Utilities, and so on. They organize the dashboard, color your subscriptions, and drive the per-category breakdowns in [Reports](/obol-user/reports/).

## Creating a category

Go to **Categories** and select **New Category**, then give it:

- **Category Name** - e.g. "Streaming".
- **Color** - a swatch used for the category and suggested for subscriptions in it.
- **Icon** - a small glyph to identify it at a glance.

## Colors flow to new subscriptions

When you add a subscription and pick a category, its **Color** is set to that category's color automatically - so everything in "Streaming" shares a look without any extra effort. You're free to override it: pick a different swatch on the subscription and your choice sticks. (This is a convenience on the *new* subscription form only; editing an existing subscription never changes its color on its own.)

## Editing and deleting

Open a category to see the subscriptions in it, then **Edit** to rename it or change its color or icon.

You can only **Delete** a category that has no subscriptions. If it still has some, Obol asks you to reassign or remove those first - this prevents accidentally orphaning your data.

## Uncategorized subscriptions

A category is optional. Subscriptions without one are grouped as **Uncategorized** on the dashboard and get their own [Uncategorized report](/obol-user/reports/#per-category-and-uncategorized).
