Obol helps you stay on top of recurring payments - streaming services, software, utilities, memberships, anything you pay for on a schedule. You add each subscription once, and Obol tracks the cost and the next renewal so nothing sneaks up on you.

## The dashboard

The home page is your dashboard - every subscription you're tracking, at a glance.

### Tiles or list

Use the **Tiles** / **List** toggle to switch how subscriptions are shown:

- **Tiles** - a colored card per subscription, with its cost, name, and when it next renews. A renewal that's due reads **Today** or **Tomorrow**; further out it reads "in N days".
- **List** - a compact table with cost per month, cost per period, and the next payment date. Click a column to sort.

### Grouping and sorting

- **Group by category** collects subscriptions under their category headings; toggle it off for one flat set.
- **Sort** by Name, Renewal, Monthly cost, or Cost.
- **Show archived** brings archived subscriptions back into view (see [Subscriptions → Archiving](/obol-user/subscriptions/#archiving-and-deleting)).

### The totals panel

At the top of the dashboard, a summary panel shows one of two views:

- **Global totals** - how much you're setting aside in total, shown per week, per month, and per year.
- **Remaining** - how much is still due in the current period (this week / month / year) - a running "what's left to pay" figure.

Switch between them with the **Global totals** / **Remaining** toggle.

## Add your first subscription

1. Select **New subscription** on the dashboard.
2. Fill in the name, cost, and next renewal date (the rest is optional).
3. Save.

That's it - it appears on the dashboard and Obol starts counting down to its renewal. The full set of options is covered in **[Subscriptions](/obol-user/subscriptions/)**.
