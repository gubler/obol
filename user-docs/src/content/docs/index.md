Welcome to **Obol** - a simple way to keep track of your subscriptions, what they cost, and when they renew.

This guide walks through the everyday flows: adding subscriptions, organizing them into categories, recording payments, and reading the reports. The full guide lands as the pages are written; for now this is the home of the user docs.
