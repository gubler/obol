title: Reports
description: See where your money goes, by category and over time.
---

The **Reports** page turns your subscriptions into a picture of your recurring spending. Everything is expressed as your **obligation** - what you're committed to paying, normalized so subscriptions on different cycles (weekly, monthly, yearly) can be compared side by side.

## Monthly obligation by category

A breakdown of your total monthly obligation across categories, shown as a pie with the figures alongside. It answers "where is my subscription money going?" at a glance. Add an active subscription and it shows up here.

## Obligation over time

A line chart of your total obligation, with a **Weekly / Monthly / Yearly** toggle so you can read it at whatever cadence makes sense. Use it to see how your commitments are trending as you add and cancel subscriptions.

## Per-category and uncategorized

- Select a category to open its own report - a **monthly obligation by subscription** breakdown for just that category.
- Subscriptions without a category roll up into the **Uncategorized** report, reached the same way.

## A note on amounts

When subscriptions are in different [currencies](/obol-user/currencies/), Obol converts them to a single display currency so the totals add up. A converted total is marked as approximate, since exchange rates move.
